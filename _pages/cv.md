## Positions

#### Postdoctoral researcher, Massachusetts Institute of Technology

*Research Laboratory of Electronics. Mar 2023 to present.*

Membrane-based active photonics: electro-optic modulators on complex-oxide
membranes, and III-V and III-N lasers and resonant-cavity LEDs integrated onto
CMOS-compatible platforms. Photonic integrated circuit testing and heterogeneous
device integration for quantum information science. Multi-physics design-to-fab:
device and fabrication-window definition by coupled simulation, CMOS foundry
tape-out, and measurement read-out. In the Jeehwan Kim group, co-advised by
Dirk R. Englund on the CMOS integrated photonics work.
