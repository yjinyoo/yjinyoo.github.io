### Three user corrections

1. **Do not put Korean in public code.** Not just wording: the tokenizer was `[A-Za-z0-9\uAC00-\uD7A3]+`.
   Characters left out never become tokens at all, so **they do not even show up as "none" in the results**; the score just
   drops silently. Changed to Unicode word characters; only the vocabulary exclusion list is an English default + `stopwords.txt` beside it.
   Keep only the structure; the language is up to the user of the code.
2. **Filler.** A sentence like `Most of what runs the loop is project code and stays private`
   gives the reader nothing and only announces that private code exists.
3. **Jargon without explanation.** `recall@K`. Dropped the term and wrote what it does.

→ Went over the five READMEs by the same criteria. **The two written last session (471 and 537 words) had no defects;
only the three written today (860, 1005, 823) did.** Removed: five "sentences announcing in advance why the next sentence matters,"
one paragraph repeating the same thing, one unexplained term. Not length, but **every spot that tried to guide
the reader** was filler.
